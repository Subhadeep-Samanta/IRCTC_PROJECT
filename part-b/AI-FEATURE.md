# AI Feature Proposal

## AI Travel Assistant

### Problem

Users struggle to understand quotas, waitlists, refunds, and booking options.

### Proposed Solution

An AI-powered assistant integrated into IRCTC.

### Features

- Natural language train search.
- PNR explanation.
- Waitlist prediction.
- Refund guidance.
- Tatkal booking assistance.
- Multi-language support.

### Example Queries

"Find me the fastest train from Delhi to Mumbai."

"What are my refund charges if I cancel now?"

"Will my waitlist ticket likely confirm?"

### Expected Benefits

- Faster task completion.
- Better support for first-time users.
- Reduced customer support load.
